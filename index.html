Methodology

Four Stages to Continuous Inventory Precision

From first SKU intake to automated replenishment cycles, every operation follows an effortless standard workflow.

01

Catalog Onboarding

Record names, SKUs, wholesale purchase costs, retail selling margins, and set customized safety reorder thresholds for each product.

02

Live Balance Logging

Every inventory deduction updates immediately in local storage. Category views provide an instant snapshot of on-hand floor stock.

03

Restock Alerts Triggered

When stock drops below predefined minimum safety numbers, STOCKFLOW flags warnings 48 hours prior to shelf depletion.

04

PO Receiving & Credit

Generate itemized supplier purchase orders, inspect incoming goods, log partial or full quantities, and automatically restore shelf balances.
