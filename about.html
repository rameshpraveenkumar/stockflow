The Story Behind the Software

The Spreadsheet Trap vs. The Enterprise Nightmare

Every retail owner starts with a simple spreadsheet. For the first fifty items, it works. But once catalog items grow into the hundreds, with multiple suppliers, fluctuating costs, and varying delivery schedules, the spreadsheet breaks down.

Stock counts go out of sync. Fast-selling favorites suddenly stock out on a busy Saturday. Slow-moving products tie up thousands of dollars of cash on back-room shelves.

When store owners look for solutions, they face two extremes: clunky legacy POS add-ons that barely handle multi-item receiving, or gargantuan enterprise ERP systems costing thousands per month that require an IT department to configure.

STOCKFLOW was created to bridge this exact gap: an intelligent, agile inventory operating system designed specifically for the daily rhythm of retail.

What Sets STOCKFLOW Apart

Designed from the ground up for retailers, not generic factories or 3PL freight brokers.

Retail-Centric Architecture

Built around SKUs, retail barcodes, minimum shelf thresholds, and supplier lead times — not industrial bill-of-materials or freight pallets.

Zero Latency Interface

Lightweight, highly optimized screens that load instantly on store iPads, mobile barcode scanners, or checkout counter laptops.

Proactive Low-Stock Alerts

Automated alerts flag items before shelves go empty, giving you ample time to generate and dispatch purchase orders to suppliers.

Integrated Supplier Directory

Never dig through an email inbox for supplier terms, phone numbers, or rep contacts. Every vendor is linked directly to their products.

Single-Increment Receiving

Engineered to prevent accidental duplicate counts when shipments arrive partially fulfilled. Stock totals update reliably on delivery.

Transparent Flat Pricing

No mandatory 5-figure onboarding retainers, no percentage cuts of your sales, and no per-terminal hidden penalty fees.
