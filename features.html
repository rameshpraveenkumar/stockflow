Module 03

Purchase Orders & Stock Receiving

Eliminate disorganized vendor invoices. Issue itemized purchase orders with multiple product rows, expected delivery dates, and real-time total calculations. When deliveries arrive, record partial or full quantities with exact inventory balance incrementation.

  • Multi-item line builder with auto-calculated subtotals and totals
  • Status pipeline: Draft, Pending, Partially Received, Received, Cancelled
  • Guaranteed single-increment stock logic preventing duplicate counts

Module 04

Low-Stock Alerts & Auto Reordering

Never be caught off-guard by a depleted shelf again. STOCKFLOW compares current on-hand units directly against your custom threshold levels. When items hit critical danger zones, create a purchase order directly from the alert modal in a single click.

  • Dynamic safety threshold detection calculated from live inventory
  • One-click purchase order generation pre-filled with supplier and suggested quantities
  • Estimated restocking cost preview to manage cash flow
