Role-Based Workspaces

Explore Your Dashboard

Every team member gets the right workspace—full purchasing and valuation control for store administrators, or focused shelf execution for floor associates.

Store Manager / Admin

Admin Dashboard

Built for store owners and managers overseeing the entire catalog, supplier procurement, valuation margins, and stock thresholds.

  • Full product catalog, category & supplier CRUD management
  • Purchase order approvals, 1-click reorder & threshold tuning
  • Wholesale vs retail valuation charts and exportable reports

Store Associate / Floor Staff

User Dashboard

Focused daily workspace designed for store associates handling aisle counts, low-stock restock checks, and receiving dock deliveries.

  • Quick shelf stock counter with instant + / - quantity updates
  • Interactive shift restock checklist for priority low-stock SKUs
  • Receiving dock delivery verification to check in inbound stock
